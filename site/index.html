Questions

Does my child need an account?

No. Children never get an account. A grown-up sets up the list, then hands over their own phone in Kid Mode, which locks behind a PIN or Face ID.

Do family members need to download the app?

No. You share a link, and they open it in any browser to see the list, claim things and buy from the shop.

Does Biro sell anything?

No. Biro shows the price and a link, and you buy from the shop itself. Biro never handles money, not even for group gifts.

How much does it cost?

The beta is free. We may earn a small commission from some shops when you buy through a link, which never changes the price you pay.

What do you do with my email?

We use it only to send your beta invite and a few updates about Biro. We never sell or share it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
