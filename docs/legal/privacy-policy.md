# Biro privacy policy

*Beta version. Effective 29 October 2026.*

Biro is a wish list app styled like an old printed gift catalogue. A grown-up sets up a list, children can circle things they'd like, and family and friends can see the list and agree who buys what.

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It's written to be read, so it's short.

**Who we are:** Biro is run by Luke Cartwright, in the UK. We're the "controller" of your information under UK and EU data protection law. To reach us about anything here, email zoneflossy@gmail.com.

## The short version

- **Children never have accounts.** Only a grown-up (18 or over) can set up Biro. Kid Mode is a locked screen on the grown-up's own phone.
- **We collect as little as we can,** and none of it is used for advertising.
- **No ads, no tracking, no selling data.** Not in Kid Mode, and not anywhere else either.
- **You can delete everything** from Settings, or by emailing us.

## What we collect, and why

| What | From whom | Why | Legal basis (UK/EU) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | The account holder | Signing in, and sending the order slip and "list finished" emails | Contract |
| The child's first name (or a nickname) and an age band, such as "6–8" | The account holder types it in | Showing "Ellie's Christmas Book" and setting sensible defaults, like hiding prices for younger children | Contract |
| The list: what's circled, star ratings, budget, and items added from links | The account holder and the child, in Kid Mode | This *is* the service | Contract |

Sent messages are deleted after 7 days. Turn notifications off in the phone's Settings at any time. | Consent |
| A backup code, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form | Made when the account holder asks for one | Moving the list to a new phone | Contract |
| A random sign-in ID for givers | Created automatically when a giver opens the link | So a giver can come back and change their claim. Givers don't need an email or password. | Legitimate interests |
| Crash reports (device model, app version, what went wrong) | Automatically | Fixing bugs. They don't contain list contents, names or emails. | Legitimate interests |
| Waitlist email address | Anyone who joins the waitlist on our website | Telling you when Biro opens. Nothing else. | Consent |

**Things we don't collect:**
- your contacts or address book
- your location
- payment details (Biro never handles money, including group gifts)
- the child's full name, date of birth, photo or voice recordings

**The parent PIN** stays on your phone, scrambled (hashed), in the phone's secure storage. It never leaves the device. Face ID and fingerprint unlocking are handled by your phone; Biro only learns "yes" or "no".

**Voice search** uses your phone's own speech recognition (Apple or Google). Biro receives the words, not the sound, and doesn't keep them after the search. Depending on your phone's settings, Apple or Google may process the audio under their own privacy policies.

## Children

Biro is designed so that a child can use it under a grown-up's supervision, without the child giving us any personal information. In Kid Mode:

- the child can only search, browse and circle
- there are no links out to shops, no settings, no ads and no in-app purchases
- getting out of Kid Mode needs the grown-up's PIN or Face ID
- the child never sees who is buying what

We follow the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the "Children's Code") and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Under COPPA, the account holder gives consent for their own household. If you believe a child has set up an account themselves, email zoneflossy@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

## Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run Biro. They may only use your information to provide their service to us.

| Provider | What for | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database and sign-in | London, UK |
| An email provider (to be chosen before Biro sends any email) | Sending order slips and notifications | We'll name it here before it's used |
| Expo / Apple / Google | Delivering push notifications | US and worldwide |

**People on the list.** Anyone with your list's share link can see the list, so share it with people you trust. You can make a new link at any time, which stops the old one working.

**Surprise mode** is optional. When it's on, the account holder sees "taken care of" (or nothing at all) instead of who claimed what. When it's off, which is the default, the account holder sees each giver's display name next to what they've claimed or bought.

**Shop links.** When you tap through to a shop, you leave Biro, and that shop's own privacy policy applies. Some links may later be affiliate links, which earn Biro a small commission at no cost to you. We'll say so clearly where that happens, and never in Kid Mode.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it for advertising.

## International transfers

Some providers are outside the UK and EU. Where they are, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep it (proposed)

- **Lists** are deleted 12 months after the event date (for example 25 December), or 12 months after the list was last opened, whichever is later.
- **Your account** is kept until you delete it.
- **Waitlist emails** are deleted once the waitlist closes, or as soon as you unsubscribe.
- **Crash reports** are deleted after 90 days.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:
- show you what we hold
- correct it
- delete it
- send it to you in a portable format
- stop using it, or restrict how we use it

You can delete your account and every list from **Settings → Delete my account**, or by emailing zoneflossy@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)) or your local data protection authority.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell account holders by email before the change takes effect.
