# Testing Biro: a guide for family

Thank you for helping test Biro before Christmas! Biro is a wish list styled like the old Argos catalogue. Children circle what they'd like, and the family agree who buys what, so nobody ends up with two of the same thing.

It's a test version, so things will go wrong. That's the point. **When something looks odd, breaks or confuses you, tell Luke.** Even "I didn't understand this button" is useful.

## How to tell Luke something

- **In the app:** Settings → Account → **Something not right? Tell Luke**. It opens an email with your phone's details already filled in, so you only need to say what happened.
- **On the family link:** tap **Something not right? Tell Luke** at the bottom of the page.
- **Or** email [zoneflossy@gmail.com](mailto:zoneflossy@gmail.com), or just message him.

The most helpful reports say **what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead.** A screenshot is gold.

## If you're a parent, with the app

Try these, in roughly this order:

1. **Set up a list.** Choose the occasion, who's circling (your child, or you), who's buying, and a budget.
2. **Circle things.** Tap a product to circle it, tap again for ★★, again for ★★★, and a fourth time to rub it out. Swipe sideways to turn the page, slowly and quickly.
3. **Hand it to your child.** Tap **Hand to [name]** to start Kid Mode. Set a PIN when asked. Let them circle, search (🔍) and browse. Then get back out with your PIN.
4. **Add something from a shop.** Tap **Add from a link**, paste a product link from any shop, and check the name and price it finds.
5. **Share with family.** Tap **Giver link** and send it to someone, on WhatsApp for example. Watch the list update when they claim something.
6. **Try surprise mode.** In Settings, change surprise mode and see what you can and can't see about who's buying.
7. **Make a backup code.** Settings → Account → **Show my backup code**. Write it down. It's how you get your list back if you change phones.

If you have the full test app (not Expo Go), also try:
- the 🎤 microphone in Kid Mode ("dinosaurs!")
- sharing a product page from Safari or Chrome to **Biro**
- Settings → Account → **Tell me when someone gets a gift**, then have someone claim something

## If you're family, with the link

You don't need the app or an account. Just open the link you were sent.

1. **Say who you are.** Pick a name everyone will recognise ("Nan", "Uncle Dave").
2. **Claim something.** Tap **Claim it**. It's held for you for 48 hours while you buy it, and everyone else sees it's taken.
3. Tap **I've bought it ✓** once you've ordered it, or **Release** if you change your mind.
4. **Chip in together.** For a big present, tap **Make it a group gift** and pledge an amount. Biro never handles money, so you sort that out between you.

Things worth checking: does the list update when someone else claims something? Is anything confusing? Do the prices and shop links look right?

## What we know isn't finished yet

- **Product photos.** The catalogue uses pictures (emoji) for now. Real photos arrive once the shops' partner programmes approve Biro.
- **Prices** were checked on 29 October, and shops change them. Always check the price on the shop's site before buying.
- **Emails** (like the "order slip" receipt) aren't switched on yet.
- **Signing in with an email** isn't switched on yet. Use the backup code to move phones.

## Your privacy

Biro collects as little as it can, has no ads and no tracking, and never collects anything from children. The details are in the [privacy policy](privacy.html). You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Account.
