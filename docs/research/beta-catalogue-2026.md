# Beta catalogue research: Christmas 2026 (draft)

**Status:** first pass, 29 October 2026. **36 products** with a reported price and a source, out of a target of about 150 (issue #17). The same products, with sources and suggested pages, are in [`beta-catalogue-2026.json`](beta-catalogue-2026.json).

## How this was gathered, and its limits

- These are the retailers' own published "top toys for Christmas 2026" lists, as reported by the press. Where possible, each product is backed by two or more outlets.
- **Prices are as published on the date shown, not live.** Retailers mark things down often before Christmas. For example, the Hot Wheels T-Rex Garage was reported at £130 on the Argos list and £90 in an Argos sale.
- The research environment's network blocks the retailer and news sites themselves. Products come from search-engine summaries of those pages. **No product page links have been verified yet**, so none are included. Each product records its retailer and a search phrase instead.
- Products for adults (for example the £200 Meccano Pro F1 set and the LEGO Doctor Doom bust, both 18+) are left out, because this is a kids' catalogue.
- The toy industry's official **DreamToys** list is usually announced in mid-November, and it's worth adding when it lands.

## Next steps to reach about 150

1. **Unblock the network** for retailer sites (argos.co.uk, smythstoys.com, thetoyshop.com, hamleys.com, johnlewis.com), so each product's page, current price and age range can be checked, and so the Entertainer and Hamleys items without prices can be priced.
2. **Fill the thin pages** with category research: craft brands (Crayola, Galt, Djeco), scooters and bikes (Micro, Frog), board games, science kits, books.
3. **Add DreamToys 2026** when it's announced in mid-November.
4. **Long term:** affiliate product feeds (Awin and others) replace hand research with live prices, stock and proper product links (brief section 8.1).
